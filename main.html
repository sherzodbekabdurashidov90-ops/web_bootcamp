How to Make Fresh Lemonade

What You Need

  • 1 cup fresh lemon juice (about 4–6 lemons)
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup water (for simple syrup)
  • 3 to 4 cups cold water
  • Ice cubes

Steps

  1. Prepare simple syrup by heating sugar and 1 cup water in a small saucepan until sugar dissolves.
  2. Allow syrup to cool down completely; never mix hot syrup directly with ice as it dilutes quickly.
  3. Juice the fresh lemons until you have exactly one cup of juice.
  4. Combine simple syrup, lemon juice, and cold water in a large pitcher and stir thoroughly.
  5. Refrigerate until chilled, serve over ice cubes, and enjoy immediately!
